# Bress-On Street Photo Scoring Master

Creator: [胶着状态 In A Film State on Xiaohongshu](https://xhslink.cn/m/ArZr9m8zJZ1) · [@j1mmywoo on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/j1mmywoo/)

An efficient Codex skill for scoring and critiquing street photographs with a transparent 100-point system. “Bress-On” is an English wordplay inspired by the Chinese name “布列紧,” a playful echo of “布列松.”

## How scoring works

Each photograph is evaluated in six areas:

| Category | Points | What it measures |
|---|---:|---|
| Moment and human meaning | 25 | Timing, gesture, expression, interaction, and narrative value |
| Composition and visual organization | 20 | Balance, subject placement, frame edges, hierarchy, and space |
| Layering and context | 15 | Relationships among subjects, depth, and environment |
| Light, tonality, and color | 15 | Light quality, tonal separation, color, and atmosphere |
| Originality and authorial voice | 15 | Personal viewpoint, surprise, ambiguity, and avoidance of clichés |
| Technical execution | 10 | Focus, exposure, motion, and processing in service of the image |

The evaluator first identifies the intended subject and moment, reads the whole frame, scores each category, checks for distractions or technical failures, and then gives one strength, one weakness, and one practical improvement.

Score guide: 90–100 exceptional; 85–89 strong; 80–84 good; 70–79 competent; 60–69 weak; below 60 reject.

## Install in Codex

```bash
git clone https://github.com/J1mmyWoo/bress-on-street-photo-scoring-master.git ~/.codex/skills/bress-on-street-photo-scoring-master
```

Restart Codex, attach your photographs, and ask: `Use Bress-On Street Photo Scoring Master to score these photos.`
